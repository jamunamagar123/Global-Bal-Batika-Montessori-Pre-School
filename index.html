A little day, a big story

What Does a Day at Bal Batika Look Like?

From good mornings to happy goodbyes — every moment is gently guided.

  1. Morning

    🌞 Good Morning

    Children arrive, greet their friends and settle into their classroom.

  2. Explore

    🧩 Explore & Learn

    Hands-on Montessori activities chosen by each child, guided with care.

  3. Create

    🎨 Create

    Art, craft and creative expression — paint, glue, build and imagine.

  4. Move

    🎵 Move & Sing

    Music, movement, rhythm and lots of joyful singing together.

  5. Outdoors

    🌿 Outdoor Adventure

    Play, climb, run and explore in the fresh air of Pokhara.

  6. Home time

    🌙 Happy Goodbye

    Going home with a new story to tell — and a bigger smile.
